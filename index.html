Lever: Firm L's debt

How these are calculated
  • EBIT = sales − operating costs. Interest = rate × debt. EBT = EBIT − interest.
  • Taxes = tax rate × EBT. Net income = EBT − taxes.
  • ROE = net income ÷ book equity, where equity = total assets − debt.
  • TIE = EBIT ÷ interest. Firm U pays no interest, so its TIE is n/a.
  • E(x) = Σ probability × x. σ(x) = √Σ probability × (x − E(x))². CV = σ ÷ E.

New-price method: buy back at the price after the announcement

Value of equity S = net income ÷ rs. Value of the firm V = S + D. Price P = V ÷ shares before the buyback. Shares repurchased = D ÷ P. WACC uses market weights: D/V × rd(1 − T) + S/V × rs.

How these are calculated
  • EBIT = sales × (1 − variable cost rate) − fixed costs. It is the same at every amount of debt.
  • Interest = rd × D. Net income = (EBIT − interest) × (1 − T). TIE = EBIT ÷ interest (n/a with no debt).
  • CDSS pays out all its earnings, so dividends per share = EPS and g = 0, which makes price = EPS ÷ rs.
  • Checks on the new-price method: EPS = rs × P, and the market-weight WACC = EBIT(1 − T) ÷ V.
  • Shown values are rounded half up; the math uses unrounded numbers throughout.
